The Supreme Law of India

Fundamental Rights Guaranteed by the Constitution

Part III of the Constitution of India guarantees fundamental liberties enforceable against the State.

Article 14

Equality Before Law

Prohibits arbitrary discrimination by public bodies and state departments. Every person enjoys equal protection of laws within India.

Article 19(1)(a)

Freedom of Speech & Expression

Protects peaceful public expression, publishing opinions, and peaceful assembly, subject only to reasonable restrictions under Art 19(2).

Article 21

Protection of Life, Liberty & Privacy

The cornerstone of Indian jurisprudence. Encompasses the right to dignity, personal liberty, clean environment, fair trial, and individual privacy.

Article 21A

Right to Education

Mandates free and compulsory elementary education for all children between the ages of 6 and 14 years across India.

Article 22

Protection Against Arrest

Mandates that anyone arrested must be informed of grounds, allowed to consult counsel of choice, and produced before a Magistrate within 24 hours.

Articles 32 & 226

Constitutional Writ Remedies

Guarantees the direct right to move the Supreme Court (Art 32) and High Courts (Art 226) for writs of Habeas Corpus, Mandamus, and Certiorari.

Frequently Asked Questions

Understanding the capabilities and boundaries of NyayaGuide.

Does NyayaGuide give formal legal advice?

No. NyayaGuide provides educational legal awareness and helps you identify relevant statutory provisions and official legal aid channels. It does not replace a licensed advocate.

How does NyayaGuide handle the new criminal laws?

The Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS 2023) came into force on 1 July 2024. For incidents on or after this date, BNS applies. For incidents prior to 1 July 2024, the Indian Penal Code applies. NyayaGuide tracks both statutes and notes cross-statutory relationships.

Does NyayaGuide record or store my voice?

No. Voice recognition is executed client-side via your browser's Web Speech API. Audio is never sent to or retained on our backend servers.
